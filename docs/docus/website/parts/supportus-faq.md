## FAQ

<details>
<summary>**How do I make the case at my company?**</summary>

Send this to whoever owns your team's tooling budget:

> We use Loki4j to ship logs from our Java services to Loki. It's a direct production dependency — if it stops being maintained, our observability pipeline is affected. It's open source, maintained by a single developer in their spare time, and downloaded ~357,000 times a month. Sponsorship starts at $100/month and funds compatibility with new Loki releases, security patches, and bug fixes. We'd also get our logo in the project README and docs. → https://loki4j.github.io/loki-logback-appender/supportus
</details>


<details>
<summary>**Can we get an invoice?**</summary>

Yes. GitHub Sponsors issues invoices for organization sponsors, so your finance team gets proper documentation for the payment.
</details>

<details>
<summary>**Will our name and logo be added automatically?**</summary>

No — nothing is published without your say. After you sponsor, you'll receive a welcome message containing an email address. Send the name or company name you'd like listed, a link if you want one, and a logo in SVG or PNG if your tier includes it. If you'd rather stay anonymous, just say so — or simply don't reply.
</details>

<details>
<summary>**We can't approve a recurring subscription - is there another way?**</summary>

There are one-time tiers alongside the monthly ones. A single payment often clears procurement far more easily than an ongoing commitment, and it carries the same recognition for twelve months.
</details>

<details>
<summary>**I'm not a company - can I still help?**</summary>

Yes, and not only with money. Sponsorship starts at $5/month, but a good bug report with a reproducer is worth just as much. So is answering a question in [Discussions](https://github.com/loki4j/loki-logback-appender/discussions), fixing a typo in these docs, writing about your Loki setup, or simply telling your team that Loki4j exists. And if you'd like your employer to sponsor, the answer "How do I make the case at my company?" is written to be forwarded.
</details>

<details>
<summary>**What does sponsorship not include?**</summary>

Sponsorship is not a support contract. It doesn't come with response-time guarantees, private support, or committed feature work — it funds the ongoing health of the project, which benefits everyone using it. If you need contractual guarantees, get in touch and we can discuss it separately.
</details>
